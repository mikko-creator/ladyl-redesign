🎒 Give Back All Year: Lady L’s School Supply Donation Drive

A YEAR-ROUND COMMUNITY INITIATIVE

When: Ongoing, 365 Days a Year

Where: Lady L Jersey City | West Side Avenue, Jersey City, NJ

Admission: Free, No purchase necessary

Who Can Participate: All community members

School Supplies Run Out. Our Support Doesn’t. 💜

The school year may begin in September, but the need for classroom essentials continues all year long!

Lady L is keeping the spirit of back-to-school giving alive with our ongoing, year-round school supply donation drive, supporting students, teachers, and families in the Jersey City community.

Whether it’s October, January, or July, there’s always an opportunity to make a difference.

🎒 What Can You Donate?

We’re collecting new, unused school supplies, including:

  • Backpacks and lunch bags
  • Notebooks and writing paper
  • Pens, pencils, and erasers
  • Crayons, markers, and colored pencils
  • Folders, binders, and pencil cases
  • Glue sticks, rulers, and other classroom essentials

💜 How to Participate

1. Gather your supplies. Choose new, unused classroom essentials you’d like to contribute.

2. Visit Lady L. Bring your donations to our Jersey City location during regular business hours.

3. Spread the word. Encourage your friends, neighbors, and family to participate.

Why We’re Doing This

We believe supporting education is an investment in our neighborhood’s future.

As a locally owned Jersey City business, Lady L is committed to creating meaningful opportunities for neighbors to come together and support one another.

No special occasion. No deadline. Just neighbors helping neighbors.

📍 Drop-Off Information

Location: Lady L Jersey City, West Side Avenue, Jersey City, NJ

Dates: Ongoing throughout the year

Cost: Free to participate. No purchase necessary.

Questions? Visit us in-store to learn more about the donation initiative.

Small Acts. Big Community Impact.

One notebook. One backpack. One thoughtful gesture at a time.

Let’s keep Jersey City giving, all year long. 💜

Lady L Jersey City, Proudly Supporting Our Community.
