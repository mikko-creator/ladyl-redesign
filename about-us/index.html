About Us

your West Side JC mom and pop Pot Shop

We started this shop to create a space where everyone feels comfortable, welcome, and taken care of.

Order for DeliveryOrder for Pickup

Lady L Cannabis is your locally owned, independent, West Side Jersey City dispensary.

Just neighbors that enjoy cannabis that are excited to share with our community the benefits and joy of legal, adult-use cannabis.

We’re easy to talk to, always listening, and open to shaping this place with you. This isn’t just our dispensary, it’s yours too.

Our Purpose

We exist to create personal and joyful cannabis shopping experiences that reflect and serve our local communitydriven by passion, purpose, and an unstoppable spirit rooted in family and community.

Our founder

Your Jersey City Dispensary, Built From the West Side Up

There's no other dispensary Jersey City has seen quite like Lady L. Our founder, Lili Hollandt, built this shop from scratch.

Our founder came to the US in 1999 and settled in Jersey City in 2011. She spent two decades in advertising, media, and operations before opening this shop. Her background shows in everything, from our team's communication to the room's atmosphere. The space is bold and warm, inspired by the energy of a well-loved home. It's not corporate or clinical, it's a living room with great products and a team that genuinely cares.

Our budtenders know their cannabis and love sharing that knowledge in an easy-to-understand way. First-timers can expect a calm, clear, no-pressure conversation. Regulars get fast, personalized service with product recommendations that fit their needs. No matter what, you'll leave knowing more than when you came in.

Mom and pop

Your fun & funky, neighborhood mom & pop dispensary

Lady L was built with women in mind, and everyone is welcome here. This is a space for the mothers juggling a hundred things, the daughters figuring out what wellness means to them, and every neighbor who just wants a great experience close to home.

Our promise is simple. When you walk through our door, you get a comfortable, welcoming experience built around you. Our budtenders meet you where you are, whether you're brand new to cannabis or you know exactly what you came in for. The vibe is warm, the knowledge is real, and judgment is unwelcome here.

Our core valueS

  • 01

    Customer and Community First

    We exist for our neighbors. Our decisions, our service, and our space are built around the people we serve.

  • 02

    Connecting People With The Plant

    We’re here to break the stigma. We believe cannabis is a powerful, personal, and natural part of wellness, and it deserves to be treated that way.

  • 03

    Always Accessible

    No gatekeeping, no attitude, just real people and open doors. We believe cannabis should be approachable, inclusive, and judgment-free.

  • 04

    Rooted in Local

    New Jersey made us who we are. As a West Side mom-and-pop, we’re proud to keep it homegrown, independent, and locally driven.

  • 05

    Curated with Care

    We’re thoughtful about what we put on our shelves, handpicking products we’d be excited to share with our own friends and family.
