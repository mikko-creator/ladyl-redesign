What's the minimum age for cannabis delivery in Hasbrouck Heights?

In New Jersey, the minimum age for purchasing adult-use cannabis is 21, and every Lady L delivery order follows that rule without exception. Age is verified when you register your account, and your driver will check your ID in person before completing the handoff.

Which forms of ID do you accept for delivery?

Lady L accepts any valid government-issued photo ID, including both New Jersey and out-of-state driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports. Your ID needs to be uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and you'll need the physical ID ready when your driver arrives.

Do I need to have a medical cannabis card to place a delivery order?

Not at all. Lady L is an adult-use cannabis microdispensary, which means any customer 21 or older can order with nothing more than a valid ID and a confirmed account.

What are the purchase limits for a Hasbrouck Heights weed delivery?

New Jersey law caps each transaction at the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis. Here's how that breaks down across product types:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What are the taxes on a Hasbrouck Heights cannabis delivery order?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis carries a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where applicable. Every charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm, so there are no surprises on the back end.

How does payment work for Lady L Cannabis delivery orders?

Typical delivery time is approximately one hour, though order volume, traffic, and distance can all affect that estimate on any given run. If things are running longer than expected, reach out to our team directly. We stay on top of delays and keep customers informed when timing shifts.

Can I make changes to my order after submitting it?

If something needs to change, contact us as soon as possible. Once we’ve dispatched your driver, modifications may not be possible, but our team will do everything we can to help. Reach out to us by phone at 201-595-0706 or through the website.

Who needs to be home when my delivery arrives?

An adult 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID must be present and available when the driver arrives. The handoff can’t be completed without someone meeting that requirement at the door, so make sure you're available during your estimated delivery window.

Are there deals available for Hasbrouck Heights weed delivery customers?

Absolutely. Our rotating delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, so it pays to check with our team or browse the promotions section on the website before to see what's currently running for delivery customers.

Loyalty points, though, work the same way for delivery as they do in-store: a dollar spent on Hasbrouck Heights weed delivery is a point earned, and every 100 points converts to $5 in store credit. On top of that, Lady L runs dedicated discount programs for specific groups, including first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several others.

Should I tip the driver?
