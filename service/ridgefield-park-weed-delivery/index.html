What's the minimum age for weed delivery in Ridgefield Park?

New Jersey's adult-use cannabis rules are clear: you must be 21 or older to place a delivery order. Age verification happens at account setup, and your driver will confirm your ID at the door before handing over your order. There are no exceptions to this requirement.

What kind of ID do I need for a Lady L delivery order?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID will work. That includes driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are welcome. Your ID must be uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and you'll need the physical ID on hand when your driver arrives.

Is a medical cannabis card required to place a delivery order?

Nope, no medical card needed. Lady L operates as an adult-use dispensary, which means any customer 21 or older can request delivery. A valid ID and a confirmed Lady L account are all that's required to get started.

How much cannabis can be included in a single delivery?

New Jersey law allows us to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis per transaction. That can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis delivery orders in New Jersey?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey carry a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50 per ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). These taxes apply to delivery orders just as they do to in-store purchases, and all applicable charges are shown at checkout before you confirm.

What should I do if my delivery is taking longer than expected?

Typical delivery time runs approximately one hour, though traffic, order volume, and distance can all affect timing. If your order is running longer than expected, reach out to our team. We stay on top of delays and communicate proactively to keep every order moving as smoothly as possible.

Can I make changes or cancel my order after it's been placed?

Get in touch with us as quickly as possible if something needs to change. Once an order has been dispatched, modifications may not be possible, but our team will do everything we can to help. Reach us by phone at 201-595-0706 or through the website.

Does someone need to be home when my order arrives?

Yes, someone 21 or older must be present and ready to show a valid government-issued ID when the driver arrives. If no one is available at the time of delivery, the order can’t be completed, so make sure you're available during your estimated delivery window to avoid any hiccups.

Are there any deals or discounts available on Ridgefield Park weed delivery orders?

Delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, but loyalty points apply to every delivery order at the same rate as in-store purchases. Every dollar spent earns one point, and every 100 points equals $5 in store credit.

Ridgefield Park residents can also take advantage of discount programs for first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several other groups. Deals rotate regularly, so check the promotions section of our site or reach out to the team for what's currently active.

Should I tip my Lady L delivery driver?

Tipping isn't required, but it's always a welcome gesture. Our drivers are trained professionals who go out of their way to make every delivery smooth, discreet, and on time. For the reliable weed delivery Ridgefield Park residents appreciate, a tip of 15 to 20% is a great way to show it.
