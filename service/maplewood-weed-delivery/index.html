Before or After Your Delivery Order

Maplewood earns its reputation one block at a time. The Village district along Maplewood Avenue between Baker and Durand Streets is home to a beloved independent bookstore, award-winning restaurants, boutiques, and a craft brewery, all within easy walking distance of Maplewood Station. The Wyoming and Hilton neighborhoods offer a quieter residential counterpoint, while Springfield Avenue adds its own commercial character to the mix. For weed delivery Maplewood, NJ, residents count on, Lady L keeps things ready when you are, whether you'll be heading out to one of your favorite local spots or you’re settled in for the day.

  • Maplewood Memorial Park

    Maplewood Memorial Park is a 25-acre historic landmark at the center of town, designed in the early twentieth century by the prominent landscape architecture firms Brinley & Holbrook and the Olmsted Brothers. Listed on both the State and National Registers of Historic Places, the park features broad greenways, a pond, rustic bridges over a channelized river, an amphitheater, ballfields, and courts, all within a picturesque landscape that has anchored community life for over a century. The Maplewood Memorial Park Conservancy stewards the ongoing restoration and improvement of the park.

  • Maplewood Village

    Maplewood Village is the town's walkable downtown commercial district, concentrated along Maplewood Avenue in the blocks surrounding the NJ Transit rail station. The district, listed on the National Register of Historic Places, is home to an eclectic mix of independently owned shops, galleries, restaurants, and cafes, including the beloved indie bookseller Words Bookstore, the French fine dining institution Lorena's, and Perch Home & Co, a curated home goods shop with a cafe inside. The Maplewood Village Alliance keeps the district active through an annual calendar of events, including art walks, music festivals, sidewalk sales, and community celebrations.

  • Bird Barn Kitchen

    Barn Bird Kitchen at 147 Maplewood Avenue is a locally sourced fried-chicken specialist with a menu centered on fresh, thoughtfully prepared comfort food. Signature items include the chicken sando, chicken and sweet potato waffles, jumbo tenders, yaji fries, and cornbread pudding, with catering available for larger gatherings. The warm, welcoming atmosphere and consistent quality have made it one of the more talked-about spots along the Maplewood Avenue dining corridor.

  • Cornbread Soul

    Cornbread Soul brings farm-fresh soul food to Maplewood Ave with a menu rooted in homemade recipes and high-quality, naturally sourced ingredients. Fried chicken, catfish, and cornbread anchor a lineup that balances bold, familiar flavors with a commitment to freshness, setting it apart from more generic fast-casual options. The Maplewood location is one of several across New Jersey and New York, reflecting the brand's growing following across the region.

  • Pallet Brewing Company

    Pallet Brewing Company at 30 Newark Way is Maplewood's neighborhood craft brewery, with a rotating tap list that spans crisp lagers, hazy IPAs, fruity sours, and hard seltzers. The taproom is a community gathering point in the truest sense, hosting weekly trivia nights, jazz performances, and live music events throughout the year. Private event bookings are available for groups looking for a relaxed, locally rooted venue.

  • Burgdorff Center for the Performing Arts

    The Burgdorff Center for the Performing Arts sits steps from Maplewood Village in a 1925 building that has been part of the township's cultural life since it was deeded to Maplewood in 1988. The facility houses a 100-seat black box theater and a 1,600-square-foot community room, both available for performances, classes, and private events. It serves as the primary home for the Maplewood Strollers, one of New Jersey's longest-running community theater companies, and hosts a rotating calendar of productions and cultural programming throughout the year.

How old do you need to be for cannabis delivery in Maplewood?

New Jersey's adult-use cannabis laws set the minimum at 21, and that applies to every Lady L delivery order without exception. Age is verified during account registration, and your driver will confirm your ID upon arrival before the handoff takes place.

What ID do I need for cannabis delivery?

A valid government-issued photo ID is required at two points in the process: uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and presented in person when your driver arrives. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports all qualify, and out-of-state IDs are accepted.

Is a medical cannabis card needed to place a delivery order in Maplewood?

No, you don’t need a medical card to order from Lady L. We’re a fully adult-use operation, open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed account and a valid ID are the only requirements to get started.

How much cannabis can be delivered in a single Maplewood order?

New Jersey sets the per-transaction limit at one ounce, or 28.35 grams, of usable cannabis. Here's how that breaks down across product types:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to a Maplewood cannabis delivery order?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis carries three charges: a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where the local municipality has authorized it. Every charge is broken out at checkout before your order is confirmed.

How does Lady L handle payment for Maplewood weed delivery?

Full payment must be submitted before your order is confirmed and a driver is dispatched. Lady L processes ACH payments and offers a debit option, but cash is not accepted for delivery. Set up your payment method through your Lady L account at registration, and call 201-595-0706 with any specific payment questions.

What if my delivery is taking longer than expected?

Most deliveries arrive within approximately one hour, but order volume, traffic, and distance can all affect timing on any given run. If things are taking longer than expected, reach out to our team directly. We keep tabs on every order and will give you a straight answer on where things stand.

Can I change or cancel my order after it’s confirmed?

Time matters here. Contact us as quickly as possible if anything needs to change, because once your driver is on the road, making modifications can be difficult and may not be possible. Reach us through the website or by calling 201-595-0706, and we’ll do our best to help.

Who needs to be available when my delivery arrives?

Someone 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID has to be there when the driver shows up. That's a firm requirement, not a suggestion. If no qualifying adult is available at the time of delivery, the driver can’t complete the handoff, so make sure you're reachable and ready during your estimated window.

Does Lady L offer any deals or discounts on Maplewood weed delivery?

Yes! And they're worth keeping an eye on. Our rotating delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, so the best way to know what's currently available for delivery customers is to check the promotions section on the website or reach out to our team directly.

Loyalty points stack up on every delivery order at the same rate as in-store purchases, turning every dollar spent into one point of progress toward store credit. A hundred points converts to $5, and those savings add up fast. Lady L also runs dedicated discount programs for specific groups, including first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several others.

Should I tip my Lady L delivery driver?

Tipping is never required, but it's a meaningful way to recognize a job well done. Our drivers bring professionalism and discretion to every delivery, and a tip in the 15-20% range goes a long way toward acknowledging that consistent effort.
