About flower

Flower is the dried, cured bud of the cannabis plant, the form most people picture first. It's usually ground and rolled, packed into a pipe or heated in a dry-herb vaporizer. Each strain has its own look and aroma, so it pays to read the label and ask questions.

Good to know

  1. 01Usually sold by weight, from a single gram to larger sizes such as an eighth (3.5 grams).
  2. 02Labels list cannabinoid content, often as a THC percentage.
  3. 03Ask a budtender to compare strains by aroma and style.
