The West Side

After You Visit, the West Side's Got More for You

Jersey City is one of the liveliest, most layered, and most exciting cities in New Jersey and on the East Coast. Our West Side neighborhood is a little quieter, a little more residential, and a lot more real than the tourist maps suggest. After you stop by our dispensary in Jersey City, there's plenty of the city worth exploring. Here are a few of our favorite spots.

  • 01

    Lincoln Park

    Lincoln Park sits just minutes from our shop on West Side Avenue, making it one of the most convenient places to decompress before or after your visit. The park spans 273 acres, featuring a lake, walking trails, athletic fields, and some of the most peaceful green space in Hudson County. It's a neighborhood gem that locals count on year-round for fresh air, wide-open skies, and that rare feeling of breathing room in the city.

  • 02

    Treehouse Coffee Shop

    Treehouse Coffee Shop is a West Side original, sitting just a few blocks from our door. Local couple Frank and Diana Young opened this place in 2019 with one goal: build something real for the neighborhood they love. The space feels exactly like the name suggests: warm, tucked in, and full of personality. Stop in for an espresso drink, a matcha latte, or the fan-favorite Vietnamese Red Eye before or after your visit with us.

  • 03

    Mana Contemporary

    Mana Contemporary is one of the most significant art campuses in the country, right here in Jersey City. This massive arts complex hosts rotating galleries, artist studios, performance spaces, and cultural exhibitions that pull in serious collectors and curious visitors alike. Whether you're deeply into contemporary art or just looking for something visually stunning to explore, Mana delivers an experience that's both approachable and genuinely impressive.

  • 04

    Landmark Loew's Jersey Theatre

    The Landmark Loew's Jersey is a meticulously preserved 1929 movie palace located in Journal Square. Walking inside feels like stepping into a golden era of design, with ornate details, grand arches, and a restored Wurlitzer organ that still fills the room with sound. The theatre hosts classic film screenings, live performances, and community events throughout the year. It's one of Jersey City's most beloved cultural institutions, and a must for anyone who appreciates history and beauty in the same space.

  • 05

    Razza

    Razza is widely regarded as one of the best pizzerias in the entire state. Chef Dan Richer has spent years perfecting naturally leavened dough and sourcing the finest seasonal ingredients to create pies that are as thoughtful as they are delicious. A meal at Razza is an experience, not just dinner. The atmosphere is warm, the wine list is well-chosen, and the pizza makes you rethink everything you thought you knew about what a slice could be.

  • 06

    Torico Ice Cream

    Torico Ice Cream is a West Side institution and a true neighborhood treasure. This homegrown, family-owned shop has been serving Jersey City for decades with bold, creative flavors rooted in Latin American tradition. From coconut to passionfruit to mango and beyond, every scoop tastes like it was made with love and a clear point of view. Stop in after visiting our shop for a sweet finish to your West Side adventure.

Where is Lady L Cannabis located?

We're located at 547 West Side Avenue, Jersey City, NJ 07304, right on the West Side's main corridor between Lexington Avenue and Clendenny Avenue. We're steps from Lincoln Park, easy to spot on one of the neighborhood's most active stretches, and just a quick ride from Journal Square.

How old do I have to be to visit the dispensary in Jersey City?

You must be 21 or older to enter our shop and make any adult-use cannabis purchase. Please bring a valid, government-issued ID every time you visit. We accept all valid government-issued IDs, including out-of-state IDs.

Do I need a medical card to visit Lady L?

No medical card is required. Lady L Cannabis operates as an adult-use dispensary, so any customer 21 or older can walk in and make a purchase. You don't need a medical cannabis card to shop with us.

What kinds of cannabis products do you carry?

Our dispensary Jersey City menu includes flower, pre-rolls, vapes, edibles, concentrates, and more. We carry a curated selection from trusted brands, with options at a range of price points. Browse the current menu before you visit to see what's available today. Product availability changes regularly, so we always recommend checking ahead.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various forms of payment. Please visit our store or call us at 201-595-0706 to inquire about.

What are the purchase limits at a dispensary in Jersey City, NJ?

Dispensaries are allowed to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. This can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, such as purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.

What taxes apply to cannabis in New Jersey?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey are subject to a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50 per ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). These taxes are applied at checkout.

Am I eligible for a discount at your weed dispensary Jersey City location?

Lady L offers a range of discount programs, including first-time customer discounts, veteran and military discounts, senior discounts, birthday discounts, referral discounts, and our community-based West Side Connection Discount. Deals rotate, so check with our team when you arrive or browse our rewards and loyalty page for the latest information.

Where can I legally consume cannabis in Jersey City?

Cannabis consumption is permitted in private residences in New Jersey. It is not permitted in public spaces, in vehicles, or on federal property. It's your responsibility to consume only in legal locations. Lady L does not provide on-site consumption space.

Do you offer cannabis delivery in Jersey City?

Yes! We offer delivery to customers throughout Jersey City and across a broad area of northern New Jersey. Our delivery service requires a Lady L account with a valid ID, uploaded and payment submitted in full before placement.

A $75 minimum order applies, and delivery is free on orders of $75 or more. A valid government-issued ID is required upon delivery. Estimated delivery time is approximately 1 hour.

Weed delivery runs:

  • Monday to Thursday: 2 PM to 9 PM (Last order: 8:30 PM)
  • Friday to Saturday: 2 PM to 10 PM (Last order: 9:30 PM)
  • Sunday: 2 PM to 8 PM (Last order: 7:30 PM)

All orders must be placed and confirmed before the last order cutoff time.

Delivery Minimums (Zone 1 Only)

Zone 1 includes zip codes: 07302, 07304, 07305, 07306, 07307, 07310, and 07311.

  • Orders $25-$74: A $10 delivery fee applies.
  • Orders $75+: Free delivery.

For all other zip codes, free delivery is available for orders of $75 or more.
