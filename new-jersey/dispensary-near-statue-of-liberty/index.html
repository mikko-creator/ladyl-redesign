A Day Worth Having: Explore the Statue of Liberty

The Statue of Liberty is one of the most visited landmarks on earth, and for good reason. A trip to Liberty Island is a full experience before you ever set foot back on shore. Here's what makes the visit worth planning around, and where Lady L fits into the day.

  • The Statue of Liberty National Monument

    The Statue of Liberty National Monument is managed by the National Park Service and draws millions of visitors each year. Liberty Island offers ground-level access, pedestal tours, and crown tours for those who book in advance. The views of New York Harbor from the island are unmatched, and the museum at the base tells the full story of the monument's design, construction, and meaning. Plan for at least two to three hours on the island, especially if you have pedestal or crown access.

  • A UNESCO World Heritage Site

    The Statue of Liberty holds UNESCO World Heritage Site status, recognized alongside Ellis Island as a landmark of universal significance. That designation speaks to the monument's role not just as an American symbol but as a global one. The connection between Liberty Island and Ellis Island, which sits nearby in the harbor, makes the area a natural full-day destination. Ellis Island's immigration museum is one of the most moving experiences in the New York Harbor area and pairs naturally with a visit to the Statue of Liberty.

  • Liberty State Park

    Just across the water in Jersey City, Liberty State Park offers some of the best unobstructed views of the Statue of Liberty and the Manhattan skyline available anywhere. The park is the departure point for Statue of Liberty and Ellis Island ferries, and it's a destination in its own right. Walking paths, open green space, and waterfront views make it a natural place to decompress after the ferry ride back. From Liberty State Park, Lady L is less than 10 minutes away by car.

How old do I need to be to visit a dispensary near Statue of Liberty?

You need to be 21 or older to shop at Lady L. Adult-use cannabis purchases require a valid, government-issued photo ID on every visit. We accept all valid government-issued IDs, including international IDs and those from other states. Expired and temporary IDs are never accepted.

Do I need to show ID to purchase cannabis?

Every time, no exceptions. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license or passport. Expired IDs and temporary IDs are not accepted, whether you're shopping in-store or placing a delivery order.

Do I need a medical card to shop at Lady L?

Not at all. Lady L is an adult-use dispensary. If you're 21 or older with a valid photo ID, you're ready to shop. No medical card, no extra paperwork required.

How much cannabis can I legally buy?

Dispensaries are allowed to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. This can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, such as purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis purchases in New Jersey?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey are subject to a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50 per ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All taxes are applied at checkout. Our team can walk you through the breakdown if you have questions.

What forms of payment do you accept at Lady L?

We accept cash, debit cards, pay by bank (ACH), pay by text (Apple/Google debit or credit), and Aeropay. All delivery orders require full payment (pay by bank) before the order is placed. An on-site ATM is available if needed.

Where can I legally consume cannabis?

New Jersey permits cannabis consumption in private residences. Public spaces, parks, vehicles, and federal property are all off-limits. Liberty State Park and Liberty Island are both federally managed spaces, so consumption is not permitted there. You're responsible for knowing and following the rules wherever you consume. Lady L does not have on-site consumption space.
