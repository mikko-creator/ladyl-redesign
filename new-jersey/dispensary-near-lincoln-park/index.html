A Day Worth Having: Explore Lincoln Park, NJ

Lincoln Park, NJ, offers more than most people realize. For customers making the trip to our dispensary near Lincoln Park, the journey can be a full experience in itself. Before you head south to Jersey City, there's plenty worth seeing and doing right in your own backyard.

  • Lincoln Park Community Lake

    This community gem offers swimming, kayaking, canoeing, paddleboarding, and row boating from Memorial Day through Labor Day. The 5.7-acre lake is surrounded by lush greenery and draws locals of all ages for a peaceful day on the water. It's the kind of spot that feels like a small town secret, and it's worth arriving early in the season to grab your time before it fills up.

  • Great Piece Meadows Preserve

    One of New Jersey's most significant natural preserves stretches right through Lincoln Park. This 7,100-acre freshwater swamp is open daily from sunrise to sunset for hiking, bird-watching, and wildlife viewing. Foxes, herons, deer, and kingfishers call this place home. For anyone who wants to clear their head before a long drive, a morning walk through Great Piece Meadows delivers the kind of quiet that's hard to find anywhere near the metro area.

  • Twin Willows Par 3 Golf

    Since 1963, this family-owned, 9-hole par 3 course on Ryerson Road has been one of Lincoln Park's most beloved local spots. Holes range from 80 to 180 yards, with greens protected by bunkers and rough that challenge players of every skill level. It's ideal for a quick round when time is tight, and the welcoming atmosphere keeps people coming back year after year. The Golf Academy offers clinics for adults and juniors throughout the warmer months.

  • Sunset Pub & Grill

    Set on the grounds of a small private airport on Beaver Brook Road, the Sunset Pub & Grill serves up some of Lincoln Park's best American fare alongside a view that's tough to beat. The outdoor patio overlooks the airfield, and watching small planes take off and land while working through their signature Angus burger or a plate of spiked calamari is a Lincoln Park experience all on its own. Live music Thursday through Sunday gives the place a real neighborhood-hangout energy.

  • Kenko Sushi

    Kenko Sushi on Comly Road has earned its reputation as a local favorite and the top-rated restaurant in Lincoln Park. This Japanese fusion spot keeps things focused on quality, with special rolls that are creative without being gimmicky. They operate Wednesday through Saturday and are takeout-first, so ordering ahead is the move. The rolls are fresh, the portions are solid, and the regulars are fiercely devoted.

  • Arca Restaurant & Bar

    Arca on Main Street is Lincoln Park's upscale Italian dining option, and it's the kind of place that fits a special occasion or a relaxed weeknight dinner equally well. The arched ceilings, candlelit atmosphere, and creative cocktail menu make it feel much bigger than a small-town restaurant. Branzino and the squid ink linguine are standout dishes, and the happy hour draws a solid crowd Sunday through Thursday.

How much cannabis can I legally buy near Lincoln Park, NJ?

Dispensaries are allowed to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. This can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, such as purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis purchases in New Jersey?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey are subject to a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50 per ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All taxes are applied at checkout. Our team can walk you through the breakdown if you have questions.

Where can I legally consume cannabis after shopping at a dispensary near Lincoln Park?

Cannabis consumption is permitted in private residences in New Jersey. Public spaces, vehicles, and federal property are off-limits. It's your responsibility to consume only in legal locations. Lady L does not offer on-site consumption space.
